    Three Starter Kits

    Not sure where to begin? Each kit is a combination that already works together. Pick the one closest to your idea, then swap parts in and out. Numbers refer to the list above.

    Ready to Build?

    This is a proposed list and may change before kits are handed out. Check availability with the lab before you order anything, and always check the voltage: micro:bit, Pico and ESP32 run on 3.3 V, the Uno on 5 V.
